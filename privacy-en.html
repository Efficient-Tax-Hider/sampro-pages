Privacy policy

Updated 22 November 2026. This policy describes sampro 3.0.1 for Google Chrome. Publisher: Wisdomous Coder.

Your recordings and audio processing stay on this device. sampro has no account service, analytics SDK, advertising tracker or automatic audio upload.

What is stored

Recordings and recoverable recording chunks are stored in Chrome's local extension IndexedDB. Track names, tags, selections, waveform peaks and recent metadata history accompany the audio. Settings, saved links and naming patterns are stored in Chrome extension storage.

Authorized source state is held for the browser session. The newest 300 local diagnostic events are retained. Logs can contain error details but do not include recorded audio.

Tab audio

SAMPRO records audio from the source tab when you press Record. Closing the panel does not stop an active recording. The source tab can remain authorized for monitoring with effects or another recording until you release it or close Chrome.

Network access and sharing

Optional site permissions let you control players, configure supported playback settings and pin the panel across sites. Source URLs and page titles may be processed locally to identify recordings. Imported audio and video files are processed locally.

sampro does not send recordings, preferences or diagnostic logs to a developer server. WAV exports, original files and diagnostic exports are local files you choose to save and share. Google Drive integration is not configured and no cloud backup occurs. Opening a saved external link or the configured Google destination navigates to that site, whose own privacy policy applies.

Retention and control

You can delete saved recordings, clear diagnostic logs, release sources, revoke site permissions and edit saved links in the extension. Original audio is preserved during editing and effects preview; processed exports create separate copies. Removing the extension or its Chrome profile can remove its local data. Export needed originals before uninstalling.

Browser-level synchronization, operating-system backups and sites you visit have their own behavior outside sampro. A future release that adds a remote service must disclose that behavior before it is enabled.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

SAMPRO’s use of information received from Chrome APIs follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
