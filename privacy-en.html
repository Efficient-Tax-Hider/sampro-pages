Data handled and purpose

sampro processes audio from sources you authorize or files you import to record, play, generate waveforms, select audio, apply effects and export. Your chosen audio, names and tags may contain personal information.

Source selection, player controls and media discovery use tab/frame identifiers, source titles and URLs, media URLs and content types on authorized sites. Media URLs may contain temporary access parameters. The extension does not read Chrome's history database or log keystrokes or mouse movements on websites.

Local storage and retention

Recordings and recoverable chunks are stored in the extension's IndexedDB in your Chrome profile. Names, tags, selections, waveform peaks and recent metadata history accompany recordings. Preferences, personal links and naming patterns use local extension storage.

Authorized-source state and discovery results use session storage. Discovery entries older than 30 minutes are filtered when read; this is not a promise of physical deletion at exactly 30 minutes. Up to 300 recent diagnostic events retain command names, times and error messages. Logs do not contain recorded audio bytes but may contain source-related context.

Permissions and capture

Microphone, tab and system audio require user authorization and remain subject to Chrome restrictions. Recording starts when you press Record. Closing the panel does not stop capture. Authorized sources can remain open until released or Chrome closes. A screen-sharing surface may be used to obtain audio; the extension saves audio, not video.

Network connections and sharing

sampro has no account server, analytics SDK, advertising tracker or automatic audio upload to a developer server. Recordings, preferences and diagnostic logs are not sent to such a server. There is no active Google Drive or cloud backup integration.

A direct media download contacts the selected media host or CDN. Its fetch path omits cookies and login credentials. Normal browsing and playback on the original website follow that site's own behavior. Personal links and the configured Google logo destination open external sites governed by their policies.

Original, WAV and diagnostic exports are local files you choose to save and share. Review diagnostic files before sharing. Emailing support or attaching files involves your mail provider and recipient outside the extension; avoid unnecessary sensitive material.

Your controls

You can delete recordings, clear diagnostic logs, edit links, release sources and revoke permissions. Processing does not replace original audio. Uninstalling, deleting the profile or changing extension IDs may remove or hide your library. Full backup/restore is not included; download important originals first.

Non-temporary data remains until deleted, replaced under bounded-history rules or extension storage is removed. sampro adds no separate encryption layer and does not promise protection against other users of the same profile or operating-system backups.

Future changes to data handling or remote services require corresponding policy and in-product disclosure updates before activation.
